# ReturnOps Demo Store — Returns Policy

**Policy ID:** `POL-2026-001`  
**Status:** PUBLISHED (fictional demo policy)  
**Effective:** `2026-01-01T00:00:00+07:00` onward  
**Store:** `demo-vn`  
**Scope:** demo order purchases where this policy is bound at checkout.

> This is a made-up store policy for a software portfolio. It is not actual marketplace policy or legal advice, and it grants no authorization by itself to move money.

## R-001 — Delivery prerequisite

A normal return request is evaluated for an item in `DELIVERED` state. If the item has not been delivered or delivery data is unavailable, do not promise eligibility; request clarification or escalate.

## R-002 — Standard change-of-mind window

A standard change-of-mind return must be submitted no later than **local calendar day 14 after delivery**, inclusive. Day 0 is the local delivery date; a request received on local day 15 is outside the normal window. The store business time zone is `Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh`.

## R-003 — Unused and intact packaging

Normal change-of-mind return requires the item to be `UNUSED` and packaging intact. Missing condition or package status requires clarification; a known used item is not eligible for ordinary change-of-mind return.

## R-004 — Excluded ordinary return categories

Hygiene items, customized items, digital goods and final-sale items are excluded from ordinary change-of-mind return. Other claim categories such as damage must be evaluated separately.

## R-005 — Damaged/wrong item within 30 days

Damage and wrong-item claims raised through local calendar day 30 after delivery, inclusive, require **manual human review** and an evidence reference. Evidence is reviewed by staff; it does not prove eligibility automatically.

## R-006 — Missing or out-of-window damage evidence

Claims after day 30 are escalated for human investigation, whether or not evidence is provided. At day 30 or earlier, damage or wrong-item claim with missing evidence requires more information. Neither condition grants automatic approval.

## R-007 — Disputes for ordinarily excluded categories

A normally excluded product category may still raise a damaged/wrong-item dispute, but only a human reviewer may investigate it. This is not a guarantee of a refund.

## R-008 — Amount source

The only proposed refund amount is the `paid_amount_vnd` stored for the specific order item, not a price supplied by the customer or language model. Complex coupon, shipping and tax proration are excluded from this demo.

## R-009 — Human approval always required

Every **simulated** refund requires approval by an authenticated `reviewer`, regardless of proposed amount. A customer or LLM cannot approve a refund.

## R-010 — Maximum amount

A return exceeding `5,000,000 VND` cannot generate a refund ledger entry in this MVP. Escalate for manual handling outside this simulated workflow.

## R-011 — Approval is bound to a frozen proposal

An approval is valid only for the exact request, item, policy version, proposed amount and proposal hash reviewed by the authenticated reviewer. Revisions require new review.

## R-012 — Safe execution

A simulated refund is executed only for the approved proposal while the request is in a valid status. Duplicate retries must produce at most one simulated ledger record and must not create a second financial effect.

## R-013 — Actor permissions

The customer can request a return of owned items. Reviewer decides approval. Only a restricted internal worker records a simulated refund. This demo does not connect to any actual financial provider.
